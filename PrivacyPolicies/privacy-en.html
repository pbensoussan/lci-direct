Privacy Policy

LCI Direct • Effective Date: November 12, 2026

LCI Direct ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles your information across iOS, iPadOS, macOS, and tvOS.

1. Data Collection and Use

We do not collect, store, track, or share any personal information or usage analytics. You can use the app without creating an account or providing any personal details.

2. Local Network and Apple TV Handoff

Our app allows you to send a video stream link from your iPhone, iPad, or Mac to your Apple TV. This communication occurs directly between your devices (via your local network or Apple system services) and is never transmitted to or stored on any external server controlled by us.

3. Third-Party Content and Streams

This app connects directly to publicly available video streams provided by third-party broadcasters (LCI / TF1 Group). When you play a live stream, your device connects directly to the broadcaster's servers, which may log standard connection data such as your IP address according to their own privacy policies. We do not use third-party advertising networks or analytics SDKs.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not collect or store any user data on our servers, there is no personal data to retain or delete. Any local app preferences are stored solely on your device and are removed if you uninstall the app.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: lci.direct.app@gmail.com
