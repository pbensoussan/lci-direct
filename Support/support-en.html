How to watch LCI live on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple TV.

1. Watching on iPhone, iPad, and Mac

LCI Direct lets you watch the live LCI stream natively on your Apple devices.

  • iPhone & iPad: Open the app and use the ••• menu to start playback (Play), pause, mute, or view stream details (Info...).
  • Mac: Use the Stream menu in the menu bar to control playback (Play, Pause, Mute), resize the player (Actual Size), or select Info... to view the stream link and its status.

2. Watching on Apple TV

Because the live stream requires a temporary session link, your iPhone, iPad, or Mac sends the stream link directly to your Apple TV over your local network.

  1. Make sure your Apple TV and your iPhone, iPad, or Mac are connected to the same Wi-Fi or local network.
  2. Open the LCI Direct app on your Apple TV. It will display: "Waiting for a link from your iPhone, iPad or Mac..."
  3. Open LCI Direct on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac.
  4. Open the ••• menu (iPhone/iPad) or the Stream menu (Mac) and choose Send Link to Apple TV.
  5. Once the link is received on your Apple TV, press Play to start watching.
Automatic Handoff Tip: Enable Send Link to Apple TV at Launch in the menu so the stream link is automatically sent to your Apple TV whenever you open the app on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac.

3. Stream Link Expiration & Status

Live stream links are temporary and valid for a limited time. You can check the exact expiration time anytime by opening Info... in the ••• or Stream menu ("Link valid until...").

If your Apple TV warns you that "The link is about to expire" or "This link has expired", simply open LCI Direct on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac and choose Send Link to Apple TV again to refresh the stream.

4. Troubleshooting

"Could not reach Apple TV"

  • Verify that the LCI Direct app is currently open and active on your Apple TV screen.
  • Confirm that both devices are on the exact same local network (and not separated by a guest Wi-Fi network or an active VPN).
  • On iPhone, iPad, and Mac, ensure Local Network permission is enabled for LCI Direct in your system privacy settings.

Playback Issues

  • Check Info... to verify the stream status and confirm the link has not expired.
  • Ensure you have an active internet connection and that no network filter is blocking access to the broadcaster's stream.
